HubVibe builds infrastructure for a world where software can discover services, communicate with other systems, and transact autonomously.

We’re building for the emerging machine economy — where agents and applications can act on behalf of people and organizations, complete real work, and pay per use without requiring a human to stand between every transaction.

Our focus is simple: make machine-to-machine interaction reliable, programmable, and economically useful.
