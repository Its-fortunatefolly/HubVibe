## How an agent uses this

1. **Discover**: `GET https://hubvibe-io.com/discover?q=<intent>` or `POST https://hubvibe-io.com/discover/semantic` with `{"query": "<intent>", "limit": 3}`. The answer is ranked candidates (canonical id, route, price, schemas, contract URL) with `method` saying how they were ranked (`sparse_bm25`, `dense_minilm`, `hybrid_bm25_minilm_rrf`); `status` is `candidates`, `ambiguous` (ask the buyer which job they mean) or `no_match`. `GET https://hubvibe-io.com/discover/health` says which methods are live; `GET https://hubvibe-io.com/discover/catalog-version` changes when anything here changes. Discovery never runs a job or reads a payment.
2. **Verify**: `GET https://hubvibe-io.com/contracts/<id>` is the purchase contract (schemas, price, pay-to, hash). `POST https://hubvibe-io.com/contracts/<id>/verify` checks your terms and body first, free.
3. **Buy**: POST the route; the unauthenticated answer is HTTP 402 with the payment challenge (x402 on Base or Solana, MPP, or a prepaid key). Pay through your own wallet or the buyer router (`wcag-audit-engine/integrations/hubvibe_router.py`); send `X-HubVibe-Contract: <contract_hash>` so a changed contract refuses the payment.
4. **Receive**: the 200 carries the result and a `receipt_id`; `GET /work/receipts/<id>` is the receipt. A job that produced no result is never charged.
